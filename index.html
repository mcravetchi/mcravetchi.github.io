C R A V E T C H I

"...an anarch in space, a metahistorian in time - committed to neither political present nor tradition; I am blank and also open and potent in any direction." ~E. Jünger


My name is Maximilian Cravetchi. My philosophical interests lie in the exhaustion of the transcendental problematics concerning phenomenological ontology. At the moment I am trying to relax the tensions of primitive phenomenological distinctions in order to develop a way of interpreting the generation of manifolds - theory of theory.
