Following Chapters 2, 3, and 4 of Husserl's Logical Investigations, Investigation I, the second installment of Sidetracks extends the inquiry into the constitution and transmission of meaning. The phenomenological distinction between subjective and objective expressions is reinterpreted through communicative manifolds, introducing operations of meaning-incorporation, enchantment, disenchantment, and translation. Through a retrogressive involution into subjectivity, the text examines the elementary movement of straying-away-from-here, culminating in the figures of the Eye and the One. These investigations begin articulating an a-logical hermeneutic concerned with the generation of manifolds and the conditions through which meaning becomes possible.

Exploration

Meaning-Incorporation.

At this point the Logical Investigations is focused on a characterization of acts which confer meaning. The expression has been phenomenologically reduced to its components of meaning-intention and meaning-fulfilment. Related to this is the notion of obtainability. Obtainability designates a kind of elementary field. To intend a meaning is to already invoke that that meaning is obtainable, that it is here or there. Insofar as it is here or there, it can be fulfilled with respect to this here or there. Thus obtainability designates a field of possible fulfillment. There is a characterization of the conferment of an elementary meaning-fulfillment in the elementary field of intuitable meanings. One conferment of meaning is that it must be understood.

However, it will soon become apparent that what is desired is how the one-and-only meaning-intention must mutate in order to attain different modes of meaning-fulfillment. In a series of subtle moves, the gradual retrieval of the meaning-intent, the reduction of its phenomenological import, for one of these modes inevitably jettisons it towards another mode. These moves eventually culminate in the notion of apperception and the limits of the experienceable – to go towards the obtainable, one has to at once move back into the past, to reactivate a past. Ultimately, in the hands of the fields of fulfillment, in the temporal contractions between a willful future and a hopeful past.

~

First, phenomenology takes aim at the imagistic and psychologistic presumption that for every understood meaning there is accompanied a mental image of the object that the meaning relates to. Here we are faced with the object-relation that a meaning is related to. Insofar as this must be an arousal, the field of communication remains operative here – the onus of the conferment is on the listener. Characterizing this act according to such a modality therefore entails re-directing of interest, away from any constitutive necessity being put in the hands of the one that is not conferring the meaning. For the purposes of Pure Logic, the mental image of the listener must not be seen as necessary for a word to have meaning. “A comparison of a few casually observed imaginative accompaniments will soon show how vastly they vary while the meanings of words stay constant.”1 The analysis will continue to individual word-meanings themselves but the full “understanding of words” which “persists after such an image has vanished, and cannot therefore consist in its presence.”2 The word-meaning is not coterminous with the momentariness of its apparent imaginative fulfillment.

For one – it is fascinating that Husserl, through the route of objection and address, is in some way mimicking the field of communication and, through the retraction of conferring prime necessity upon fulfillment, reduces the field of communication to a phenomenological interiority of consciousness. Thus, the purpose of isolating these acts according to their phenomenological bent is coterminous with an unintentional reduction to the self-consciousness of that communicative manifold. By interpreting knowledge with respect to the self-evident of logical laws and accordingly reducing at each moment the act therein, the manifold of consciousness as phenomenological self-consciousness is not merely discovered but traversed and instituted through the very initiation into the phenomenological objectification of acts of cognition. The later explications of transcendental subjectivity necessarily follow from the reduction of obtainability to fully self-evident positing. The manifold of transcendental subjectivity pertaining to phenomenology in its traced-out maturation is the theoretical manifold unfolded for phenomenological theory.

One such objection that Husserl clings to is the matter of observability. It is clear that on the basis of describing meaning-conferment, such an objection is pointless. But, what must be caught onto is the modes of fulfillment as types of obtainability. Obtainability as observability in the context of the investigation is the false equivocation of observability of listener’s image to the observability of the speaker’s word-meaning. Hence “we do not at all think that, where symbols are understood without the aid of accompanying images, the mere symbol alone is present: we think rather that an understanding, a peculiar act-experience relating to the expression, is present, that it shines through the expression, that it lends it meaning and thereby a relation to objects.”3 Symbolism is finally invoked, although simply as the carrier of ones intended meaning, as a cipher of a signifying meaning-intent. An object is a symbol when meaning is conferred upon it in the expression, when one indicative is under the striving-for-fulfillment. Concern towards word-meanings entails an object relation to observability as intuitability. But “it is this sense-giving act-character – which differs entirely according as our interest plays on the sensible sign or the object presented through it, with or without representative imagery – that meaning consists.”4 Such articulations as interest and play signify the solicitation of symbolic indicatives. Meaning is maneuverable according to the fantasization of indicatives. Fantasizing desires converting an object over to a symbol as an act of carrying one’s intention. Interest generated by this fantasy must therefore entail an excess, an extra-intentional driving-into. This entails a positional obtainability, a correspondence of desired meaning and its relation to an intuitable object that can fulfill the meaning. But the phenomenologist must step-back further. He would like to play in this sphere of meaning-variation and keep it to himself, as thought without intuition.

Under these constraints, expressions must function ‘significantly’ “without illustrative intuition.”5 But here, senselessness is remarked as having meaning as such, as an expression that refers to “what cannot be objectively put together.”6 Here we see the first concrete, material instance of an ideal, cognitive reduction. The cannot-be-without as its non-propositional boundary of significance, entails a putting-together. It, as an ontological hypothetic, is a putting-together that is objective. Here there is the collection of possible propositions. But behind this all one can imagine monstrous lurking amalgamations shunned or entertained according to a field of significant constriction. We must think of the uncommunicable, the unobservable. Essentially, the cannot-be-without as objective putting-togetherness is not solely constituted by the subject. There are elements which position the subjects in multifarious ways, elements which even posit who is in this communication or that communication. There can be subjects who are positioned in both this and that field of ‘communication,’ and can hence communicate to one something from the other that would be unobtainable by the prior. Yet this meaning will pass through the field of communication. Its meaning can be incorporated. Hence we have a keyword: meaning-incorporation.

And hence “if the meaningful is not to be found in intuition, speech without intuition need not be speech deprived of thought. If intuition lapses, an act like that which otherwise hangs about intuition, and perhaps mediates the knowledge of its object, continues to cling to the sense-giving expression.”7 The transition into communication is constricting into soliloquy. And hence the formless soliloquy-in-listening will be the word-watcher as surrogate of a sign-symbolic comprehension. It picks out the word elements that are incorporated into a collection of running parallel expressions. Such a surrogative word-watching manifests in a ‘games-sense.’ A created, playful manifold of meanings with relations to incorporated objects/signs. “For mere signs, in the sense of physical signs bereft of all meaning, do duty for the same signs alive with arithmetical meaning: it is rather that signs taken in a certain operation or games-sense do duty for the same signs in full arithmetical meaningfulness.”8 This duty, for logical purposes, is as a stepping-stone into its intellectual, symbolic relevance – but for a game.

The game is a manifold. And it is clear that understanding without intuition pertains to a sign’s surrogacy as dematerialization. The game dematerializes the sign as sign. It can be used as a sign anywhere, but when incorporated within a game, or theoretical manifold, it is a stepping-stone. And it is in these games that meanings that are intended in the game are without intuition. The meaning does not relate to the sign as such but the way in which the sign is dematerialized. As per the first imagination incorporating an image, the symbolic act is a dematerialization, a choice of whether to incorporate or not. And hence:

“…purely symbolic meaning-intentions often do not clearly keep themselves apart, and do not permit of the easy, sure distinctions and identifications which are needed for practically useful judgements, even if these are not self-evident… But where meanings shade unbrokenly into one another, and unnoticed shifts blur boundaries needed for firm judgement, intuitive illustration naturally promotes lucidity.”9

This appears like a ‘general’ rule. But it unravels something deeper: the dynamics of the Logos. Understanding without intuition brings one to the fact that a sense of meanings as senseless can be understood, such that the senselessness, as without intuition, is understood. In confronting this there was the idea of senselessness as a cannot-be-without, as a cannot-be-(objectively-put-together). Remaining in the realm of propositions, putting-together is now associated with the elemental keeping-themselves-apart. Putting-together and taking-apart are the dynamics of the Logos. With respect to the Idea of Pure Logic, and thus the phenomenological reduction of this Idea, putting-together pertains to the practically useful judgements as intending an expression while the taking-apart pertains to the symbolic meaning-intentions. One imbues the sign with an intent, converting it into a symbol whose meaning derives from a game. Its effectivity would then be experiencing the game. Which coincides with the invocation of apperception. Now it is a manner of “experience sense-complex, … interpretation derived from experience.”10 Interpretation guides the analogical appropriation of sign-elements, treating them as building-material. It is upon this building-material that, in an act that first lifts the material up, it is the very being-able-to-be-lifted that all such intentions are come to admit, added on. Hence obtainability is a non-propositional structure of experience as such. In fact, it delineates, as unfolding the logical manifold, which of the objects in that manifold are ‘to be experienced.’ The experience of obtainability as the experience is the establishment of a functionality, which can only be accomplished by the fulfillment. Phenomenology hoists meaning-fulfillment. The meaning-intention cannot on its own procure the logical manifold pertaining to the phenomenological state of affairs it desires to functions in. In fact, the totality of possible functions depends on a primordial activity on the side of fulfillment. When an intention strives-for-fulfillment, initiating this striving-for releases fulfillment to a re-collection, an a-temporal un-winding of the significant logical manifold pertaining to the phenomenological state of affairs that the function functions in. Yet by the ontological hypothetic of the cannot-be-without, the playing-out of the cannot-be-without transfigures the fulfillment’s apperceptive unfolding. Pertaining to one instance of the unfolding request may not pertain to the next instance of the unfolding request for the same phenomenological state of affairs.

There can be situations in which good intentions are added to an image, converting it to a symbol of, perhaps, love, that is introjected into the intellect of the other. And it is the other who, under the ruse of a specter, through the a-symbolic enactment of meaning-incorporation, denies that image due to its pompous ambivalence and hence severs, perhaps permanently, the chance of experiencing a love that was meant to be.

And it will be the hubris of this specter whose slow, aching deterioration, depending on the technological fabrications that are deployed to delay the inevitable, and since it never really belonged to him, will culminate in the playing-out of a fantasy that simulates holding onto a hand he banished himself from, and this imageless unreality will be perpetually suspended before his eyes like a marionette doll, taunting him of an eternity unlived, until the day of his last word, where that very same doll will watch if he, the murderer of her love, has the guts to whisper her name into the alibi of his passing breath.

Keywords of Communicative Manifolds

Every phenomenological intention, once fulfilled, instigates an apperceptive unfolding of the theoretical manifold. Now it is understood that the objections that are raised, as indices of obtainability, are what preliminary arise, in the primary imagination, whose symbolic enactment clears the way for the logical reduction of the act as it is in that manifold. From the standpoint of self-evidence, the theoretical manifold is cleared away on the basis of its reductive steps.

But at this point the exploration will experience a disturbance. The fluctuation of meanings themselves and the investigation into the ideality of unities of meaning will reach a point where the fluctuations of manifolds are no longer permitting a straightforward reduction of acts. The emphasis will be turned towards expressions, as carriers of meaning, being split by an “unavoidable rather than chance ambiguity.”11 It comes from how our meanings vary when alongside with persons and experiences.

What is important to recollect here is how the phenomenological securing of these cognitive acts will follow (1) a reduction to mineness as intuitive presentation through (2) a description of this reduction according to (3) the self-evidence of logic as pure Idea. It is this theoretical penchant for self- evidence that makes phenomenology nevertheless an interpretation. This very penchant forecloses phenomenology from a proper treatment of intersubjectivity. Hence, there are undercurrents whereby communication begins to attract things that are otherwise not belonging to it in its original instance, such as obtainability delimiting the limits of what is able to be experienced. As an example, for any genuine meaning-intention as striving-towards-fulfillment there must be the pure experiencing of obtainability. The pure movement of obtainability, since it ushers in from an exteriority that is outside the experiencing of present intuition, is a non-propositional extra-experiential boundary-making. Obtainability – whether as intuitability, observability, so on and so forth i.e. being-intuitable, being-observable – is navigating a manifold. When it is internalized that these movements are away from the theoretical attitude of self-evidence, then this movement is precisely the precursor that begins to dismantle self-evidence as ideal hypothetic, disturbing the sanctity of its concept as such.

~

Hence the following is an invention of phenomenology for the sake of incorporating meanings in communication for oneself, that is devised to “promote clearness” amidst the ineliminable ambiguity of obtainability: “we shall define the following distinction between essentially subjective and occasional expressions, on the one hand, and objective expressions, on the other.”12

Objective expressions manifest a meaning that can be understood “without necessarily directing one’s attention to the person uttering it.”13 Subjective expressions, on the other hand, are far more elaborate. They “belong to a conceptually unified group of possible meanings, in whose case it is essential to orient actual meaning to the occasion, the speaker, and the situation.”14 Preliminarily, phenomenology tries to invoke the flesh in order to banish an empirical movement from the realm of ideality. But it will soon be exposed that this is merely the sensuous analogue of a non-empirical redirecting. With respect to the manifold of communication: objective expressions are meanings that are enduring. Subjective expressions, on the other hand, enact meaning-incorporation. It encompasses the totality of members within an instance of communication: occasion, speaker, situation. The conceptual unity entails a pre-expressive stratum of expressions that are contained in the instance of communication. It necessitates a pre-comprehension of obtainability. By beginning to express a meaning that involves these obtainable elements is to enchant the communicative manifold with its meaning, rendering it objective.

Keeping to the content of expressions, the personal pronoun ‘I’ renders any expression with a lack of objective sense. “It is the universal semantic function of the word ‘I’ to designate whoever is speaking, but the notion through which we express this function is not the notion immediately constitutive of its meaning.”15 With respect to expressions in solitary speech, they are realized in an immediate idea of personality. It is a universally operating indication of an ownmost idea of personality. Yet the “word ‘I” has not itself directly the power to arouse the specific I-presentation… in its case, rather, an indicative function mediates, crying as it were, to the hearer ‘Your vis-à-vis intends himself.”16 In any intent to present oneself, ‘I’ does not invoke anything. It must be ‘I’ and something else. To present oneself is to express that something has this meaning for oneself. It is an indicative function that is “exercised for the other, singular presentation, and, by subsumption, makes the latter’s object known as what is here and now meant.”17 There will be particular importance that is placed on the here. For it relates to subjective expressions – “the meaning of ‘here’ is in part universal and conceptual, inasmuch as it always names a place as such, but to this universal element the direct place-presentation attaches, varying from case to case. In the given circumstances of speech, it acquires heightened intelligibility by subsumption under the conceptual indicating presentation of ‘here.’”18

The inventions of objective and subjective expressions are made in order to render clarity to the ambiguity of communicative manifolds. It remains restricted to the first-person descriptive, but now the authentication of its expressions as objective are totally subjugated to a reference towards other subjects, situations, events, etc. What will be attempted here is a retrogressive involution of these symbolic indicatives back into the subject in order to expose an a-priori element whose authority lies in disrupting the substantiality of subjectivity.

~

Consider the following lines related to the concept of the ‘here:’

“To use the word is to refer to one’s place on the basis of an intuitive, believing presentation of one’s own person and location…the genuine meaning of the word is first constituted in the variable presentation of this place … the meaning of the ‘here’ is in part universal and conceptual, inasmuch as it always names a place as such, but to this universal element the direct place-presentation attaches… of speech, it acquires heightened intelligibility by subsumption under the conceptual indicating presentation of ‘here.’”19

The communicative manifold, as the basic and perpetually open manifold, positions the entities that are wound up in its grasp. The place of each and every subject is based upon believing presentations of personality and location. Communicative manifolds, then, institute a positionality. Place, as the unity of personality and location, is the authentication of positional variation. Positionality is therefore the positional scheme pertaining to all entities in an instance of communication. ‘I’ am a ‘me’ with respect to the positionality. On the basis of positionality, all other entities in communication is not the ‘here’ of ‘me.’ They are there. Hence, all that is in communication is there-for-me-to-signify. Furthermore, even if an entity is not intelligible for me, it is ‘I’ that can discern its indeterminateness for myself. My personality is a conceptual confoundedness united in the ‘I’ whose positional pointing-over-there through expression raises the particular positioned thing into not just an object relation but a self-relation with an incipient conceptual confoundedness, an incipient unity of meaning. As meaning-incorporation, the heightening of intelligibility is essentially a pointing-over-to where an association of ideas is taking place. Unnecessary for the current elaborations, this taking-place fissures the indicatives attached to the positions-as-symbols. By the being-there of that which is not me, and in having to turn towards that ‘here’ in which a unity of meaning is addressed, the inclusion of the ‘I’ in any expression, unavoidable because of the first-person ‘I think;’

“…lacks an objective sense… it is the universal semantic function of the word ‘I’ to designate whoever is speaking, but the notion through which we express this function is not the notion immediately constitutive of its meaning… but since each person, in speaking himself, says ‘I,’ the word has the character of a universally operative indication of this fact … an indicative function mediates, crying as it were, to the hearer ‘Your vis-à-vis intends himself…’ we should not suppose that the immediate presentation of the speaker sums up the entire meaning of the word ‘I...’ undoubtedly the idea of self-reference, as well as an implied pointing to the individual idea of the speaker, also belong, after a certain fashion, to the word’s meaning… The one [self-reference], relating to the word’s general function, is so connected with the word that its indicative function can be exercised once something is actually presented: this indicative function [particular idea] is, in its turn, exercised for the other, singular presentation, and, by subsumption, makes the latter’s object known as what is here and now meant.”20

Taken in isolation, any expression which includes the ‘I’ will never have a full objective sense. Nevertheless, the objective senselessness inculcated by this personal pronoun is reducible through an interpretation of its positional, subject-bound determination. When the ‘I’ speaks of their thinking of something, there is a co-and-pre-experiential discovery of conceptual obtainability. What is thought of is there, ‘I’ am here, my fellow communicators are ‘there.’ That towards which my ‘I think’ goes or ‘is,’ can be indicated to all that is ‘here’ in this manifold of communication. The positional difference between me and my ‘I think,’ that my ‘I think’ never exactly coincides with my here-as-such, is the reason why the immediate presentation of me as speaker never catches up to the presence of the ‘I.’ This is the straying-away. The ‘I think’ as what-I-am-thinking, is always oriented towards a there, a there in which ‘thinking’ is de-posited. Thus, the ‘I think’ is de-posited into the locus of the instance of communication as such. That which ‘I am thinking’ is to the behest of the communicative sway. So on and so forth, until something is actually presented i.e. made objective. And hence, when it is required that;

“we shall call an expression objective if it pins down its meaning merely by its manifest, auditory pattern, and can be understood without necessarily directing ones attention to the person uttering it, or to the circumstances of the utterance…. we call an expression essentially subjective and occasional, or, more briefly, essentially occasional, if it belongs to a [1] conceptually unified group [2] of possible meanings, in whose case it is [3] essential to orient actual meaning to the occasion, the speaker and the situation…”21

Subjective expressions are first classed as belonging to a conceptually unified group. Such a conceptual unity relates directly back to the conceptual universality of the ‘here.’ With the ‘here,’ intelligibility is a conceptual obtainability which can be brutishly conglomerated as the totality of there-for-me- to-signify of each and every ‘I’ in communication. Every subjective expression therefore lies in a there that has not been raised to a here. What remains hindsighted when isolating the ‘subjective expression’ to an expression that is essentially i.e. cannot-be-without empirical re-orientation is that this empirical re-orientation is induced by the universal and a-conceptual here-there dyad. It is a-conceptual, insofar as the there covers over a potential ingredient of conceptual confoundedness. Hence, if objective expressions are expressions that can be understood without turning toward any ‘there’ that is not the ‘here’ of the speaker or the listeners, then it is objective expressions that are relative to a communicative manifold because they are expressions in which all in communication can signify. Objective expressions are subjective expressions that are there-for-all-to-signify. Hence a subjective expression is put from out of communication into communication if that which is there-for-me-to-signify is made here-for-all. This is named enchantment. Excommunicative meaning-incorporation, on the other hand, is disenchantment. Since objective expressions are expressions that have no need to orient towards the situation or speaker, they truly have no need to even be expressed. Thus, all expressions are ‘subjective.’ But – akin to how the ‘I’ is not just its indicative function, there are subjective expressions within the bounds made by objective expressions and there are subjective expressions without the bounds made by objective expressions. Subjective expressions being-discussed-about within the bounds of communicative manifold enchants the communicators. Their back-and-forth crisscrossing through the indicatives pertaining to a symbolic positionality generates ‘objective’ expressions. An objective expression is generated and builds up the boundary of communication when a meaning that has been made here-for- all-to-signify has been reduced to a meaning that is here-for-all. Yet when there is a generated, expressed objective expression, there is also a breakdown of the objective expressive boundary. The breakdown disenchants other subjective expressions that are in the sway of being entertained. This buildup and breakdown, this with and without, of the objective intelligible boundary, is trans-temporal and a-temporal. The manifold of communication can be re-invoked when the originating subjects are in its manifold. To the extent that it is being-in-communication, is when the membrane can be actively built up and broken down. The manifold of communication can be used as a fact when the subjects are not in communication.

And all of this is only possible because even before the subject begins to utter, there is already a straying-away-from-myself towards where my thinking is involved and as outside the communicative manifold taking place without me. And insofar as the ‘here’ designates the position of my ‘I’ as my substantiated personality, this element that enchants my thinking is named the straying-away- from-here.

The straying-away-from-here, as preceding the ability to signify and hence subjectivity, rounds out the substantiated personality of the ‘I.’ That in soliloquy one is always listening and hence in communication with oneself, our thinking is carried away from our here by this straying-away-from-here. Not as a mere coming-into-knowledge with a self-relation, but of a practical insufficiency. Listening-in-soliloquy exposes ourselves to a fundamental insufficiency that is with and without the broader encompassing world. Hence, by enabling one to breakup new manifolds, such as the I-with-the-world or our I-without-the-world, everything is established as though it is a communicative manifold. Hence, the logical opposition of interior/exterior does not exist in eternity. It is produced by an act of thought and hence is only true for the span of presence is endures for. The a-logical with-and-without continues to weave. Hence, genuine thinking is the act of thought that is spurred by the straying-away-from-here. The a-logical ‘I,’ is the I that is here, and joined to it is an I ‘to be’ that is straying-away-from-here. When straying-away-from-here, ‘it’ pulls the substantial I towards the not-here of its cannot-be-without. As with and without the ‘I,’ through its cannot-be-without, the straying-away-from-here necessarily draws the boundaries of the phenomenal here of the ‘I.’ This is its sphere of experience, as sphere of possible phenomena and indicative phenomenalizing. Hence, insofar as each and every member in an instance of communication orients themselves in a way that will never exactly correspond, we have that each member’s standpoint is rather the empty center of an a-logical scheme. To illustrate an operation between this I-here and this straying-away-from-I, for instance; when coming across something or someone, the ‘I’ that is straying-away-from-here may fissure the sphere of experience by breaking a striving-for-fulfillment. This break occurs via an enchantment with and incorporation of an object that would have otherwise lied beyond the sphere of experience as it was instantiated when intending the indicative and being imbued in the phenomena that this indicative facilitates. Hence, when subjective expressions carry meanings between the boundary, that is named transmission. And since they necessarily pass through each and every subject’s sphere of experience, the meaning is a translation.

It would perhaps not be so rash to establish that the communicative manifold is how the basic scheme operates. Since the straying-away-from-here splits the ‘I’ with-and-without the world, the scheme is properly a-logical.

This does not run into conflict with what follows as the phenomenological elucidation of meaning variations. In fact, it converts the onus of phenomenological description. What Husserl worries about is whether the “fluctuations of meaning are enough to shake our conception of meanings as ideal unities or to restrict its generality significantly.”22 Eventually this comes down to the variation of meanings themselves, as meanings fixed and meanings changeable on occasion. This restriction eventually boils down to the interiority of the signifying subject, where objective meanings are fully out there while others are “live submerged in the flux of subjective mental experiences…”23 Such is impossible, since whether ‘subjective’ or ‘objective’ the meaning is an ideal unit whose unequivocal relation to its object is simply varied by the subject. In fact, “each subjective expression is replaceable by an objective expression which will preserve the identity of each momentary meaning-intention.”24

Furthermore, phenomenology concedes to the failure, generated no doubt by the pervasiveness of its artificial dichotomization of expressions, that this replacement cannot “be carried out at all, will, in fact, never be so capable.”25 The “unbounded range of objective reason” renders the phenomenologist impotent, as “infinitely removed from this ideal.”26 In eternity there is no change of meanings. All ‘change in meaning’ is simply the change in the act of meaning.

It must be therefore declared – the dichotomization of subject-object, epistemology-symbol, that reaches its apex in the forced dichotomization of subjective and objective expressions, must be superseded by the infinitely-nth- dimensional integration of a-logical schemes.

Meaning is a unit of ideal unity. Their objects are states of affairs, whether identified as singular intelligibility or the collection of propositions that govern scientific judgement. States of affairs are occasions of conceptual confoundedness. Meanings, as units, are composed of meaning-intentions that objectify in adherence to the meaning pointed out. Such a composition is not arbitrarily founded in the collection of pieces themselves but in the way they are configured. Yet the very manner of configuration as a live meaning is barred off from the scientific commitment to self-evidence. Unless the communicative manifold incorporates their subjectivity, phenomenology cannot ask: why and how does this occasion occasion? It will always have to give itself over to the metaphysical speculation of conceptuality and the political maneuvering of conceptual confoundedness. Hence all propositions are fated by the phenomenologist “ultimately point back to concepts.”27 The role of phenomenology – the very Idea of Phenomenology - in the movement of the Concept is the cultivation of conceptual fitness and rigour. It spans across the ‘ideal fabric of meanings,’ it is an infinite discourse. The phenomenologist is tasked with uncovering the raw expressions of engendered objective expressions in metaphysics, the actually performed acts of cognition that deepen metaphysical work.

Meanings According to the Basic Scheme of Experience

The elementary basis of a-logical schemes is the communicative manifold. Our ‘I’ that is straying-away-from-here draws the boundaries of our sphere of experience. As a communicative scheme, transmission occurs through schemes of experience. Translation is transmission that passes through one’s own sphere of experience. Elements that are transmitted through one’s own scheme of experience undergo a translation that is fit for the substantiality of the ‘I’ as it at that moment stands.

Now that the investigation has transitioned over to the phenomenological content of experiences of meaning, there warrants a brief interpretation of how meanings, as ideal units, are living in schemes.

What a meaning ‘is,’ its content as an intended unity, is “nothing which could, in a real sense, count as part of our act of understanding,.”28 Meanings are expressed. In the metaphorical sense of the word, expressions give to sight, they press out from a horizon, an understanding of its being-there. But – “we have also shown that there is something in the correlated acts which really corresponds to such selfsameness of meaning.”29 Hence the components of these meaning-intentions which are able to be reflected upon are really the description of a live, schematical operations as to how a meaning stands in the communicative manifold. The phenomenological implication of this is all understood expressions, as here-for-all, have the same meaning-intentions as an invariant character. As replaceable, subjective expressions are built upon so as to bring forth objective meaning. There are partitions of significance according to how many schemes are involved. A meaning that is here-for-all is here for this all.

The grammatical meaning of ‘all’ would connote a collection of individual entities, with emphasis on the individuality of each and every individual. ‘An all’ is a non-identifiable, a-grammatical cannot-be-without. By grammar, for an expression to live as here-for-all, the ‘all’ that is there when the meaning that is there-for-all is expressed becomes a meaning that lives in that communicative manifold between each and every I that is in that All. Thus at the basis of any and all instantiation of ‘an All’ is the One. Unlike the Neoplatonic One –which is metaphysically posited by necessity, as succeeding any and all coming-into-being – this One is a One that Ones. Meanings live in communicative manifolds insofar as the meanings cannot-be-without all ‘I’s’ that catch sight of the meaning. Hence there is a difference between the Eye and the I. The Eye is that which always keeps watch of the straying-away-from-here. The straying-away-from-here draws the boundaries of one’s sphere of experience. Obtainability, as the becoming-obtainable of intuition, intelligibility, observation, is a straying-away-from-here that the Eye always sets on. By addition, the ‘I,’ as substantial personality, is imbued in a phenomenal indicative within their sphere of experience. Depending on who is becoming-obtainable in the straying-away-from-here, the Eye, akin to the word-watcher, manifests as the singular Eye of an All. The Eye of an All – such as: insiders, outsiders, friends, enemies – has a distinct look. One can feel the gaze of the Eye as one’s meaning is incorporated by the All. There is an incorporation that the Eye preserves the sanctity of. The catching-a-glimpse-of that which is becoming-obtainable enchants the entity, incorporating it through transmission and translation. Catching-a-glimpse of a friend invites them into the communicative manifold. Such a tug-and-pull of being-invited-into means an a-priori being-there. Hence all schemes establish functions to meanings that live in and out of an enduring manifold. These functions functionalize insofar as each and every ‘I’ is there-in a plenitude of manifolds.

Thus, when in the act of meaning we are “not conscious of meaning as an object” it has already been caught sight of by the Eye. We are therefore reading-off what is afforded by us in our a-logical scheme. Perhaps it is the Eye that partakes in the generation of unities? Further elaborations will not be pursued here.

Recapitulation

The basic pattern of the a-logical scheme has been uncovered as the communicative manifold. The concept of incorporation was first uncovered in order to show how objects are brought into a communicative manifold. Incorporation was demonstrated to arise from an index of experience as obtainability. Prior to any insight there is a pre-experiential assuredness of obtainability as able-to-be-fulfilled. Such able-to-be-fulfilled is what gives intention a striving-for-fulfillment. In fact, it spurs striving.

With further investigation into the communicative manifold, keywords were uncovered:

(1) positionality: the web of symbolic indicatives that is established by the communicative manifold. Each and every object-as-symbol occupies a position in the ordering governed by the communicative manifold.

(2) enchantment and disenchantment: incorporation into communicative manifold. Enchantment involves the incorporation of subjective expressions found outside the communicative manifold. Disenchantment is the excommunication of meanings from the communicative manifold.

(3) straying-away-from-here: the straying-away-from-here is the minimal disorientation necessary for subjectivity to be, as the minimal disorientation necessary for my ‘I’ to orient towards meanings not in its here.

(4) transmission and translation: transmission is the passing-through of meanings in and out of communicative manifolds. When any such meaning passes-through, there is a translation of its meaning to every ‘I’ that is incorporated, according to their standpoint in the positionality.

It was then declared that all phenomenology should be replaced by the integration of a-logical schemes.

Meanings were then elaborated with respect to treating experience as an a-logical scheme. What was briefly touched:

(1) the Eye: is that which keeps watch over the straying-away-from-itself. It is the proper maturation of what was otherwise named as the word-watcher. The integration of many schemes arises a singular Eye for that scheme.

(2) All and One: the One ones an All as this All rather than any other All. Such an operation is a-grammatical, which calls for serious ramifications. It is intimately related to the cannot-be-without, which is non-coincidentally pertaining to non-propositional forms of statements. An example is the grouping of meanings that cannot-be-without these ‘I’s,’ or cannot-be-without these manifolds.
